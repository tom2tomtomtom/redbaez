Case Study

Monigle

AI embedded into processes and culture. On the leading edge of a fast-moving space. Growth team workflows and branded presentation automation.

The Challenge

Monigle is a leading US brand consultancy with offices in Denver and New York. They were already AI-curious, but needed a strategic partner who could move beyond experimentation and embed AI into their actual working processes. The challenge was not awareness. It was execution.

Their growth team needed faster workflows for business development. Their consultants were spending hours formatting presentations into branded templates. And they needed someone who understood both the technology and the consulting culture well enough to make it stick.

What We Built

AI Strategy Embedding

A structured approach to integrating AI across Monigle's consulting processes. Not a one-off workshop, but a systematic embedding into how the team thinks and works every day.

Growth Team Workflows

Custom Claude workflows for the growth team. Prospect research, proposal drafting, competitive analysis. The business development process accelerated at every stage.

Branded PPTX Skill

Programmatic PowerPoint generation that matches Monigle's exact brand templates. 39 official layouts with correct fonts, colours, and positioning. What took half a day now takes minutes.

The Result

39 Branded slide layouts automated
Leading Edge AI adoption

Monigle went from AI-curious to AI-embedded. The growth team has faster workflows for every stage of business development. Consultants spend their time on strategic thinking, not slide formatting. And the cultural shift was the real win: AI is now part of how Monigle works, not something they are still evaluating.

"Tom has helped embed AI into our processes and culture, helping Monigle be on the leading edge in a fast-moving space."
Gunnar Jacobs, Principal, Monigle
