Case Study

Alt-Shift

24 to 36 hours freed per week. The equivalent of a full-time hire through AI automation alone. 43 people, 3 offices, one integrated intelligence system.

The Challenge

Alt-Shift is a 43-person integrated PR and creative agency operating across three Australian offices. Their team was stretched thin across media monitoring, opportunity spotting, trend tracking, and resource planning. These were not creative challenges. They were time sinks that pulled senior people away from client work and new business.

The brief was specific: find the hours hidden in manual processes and give them back to the team. Not with a new platform to learn, but with automation that runs in the background and delivers results to Slack every morning.

What We Built

Opportunity Intelligence

8 signal types monitored daily. Client moves, agency reviews, funding rounds, crisis indicators, market shifts, leadership changes, M&A activity, and regulatory signals. Daily 6am Slack alerts with actionable briefs.

Cultural Trend Detection

Automated scanning across platforms for emerging cultural trends relevant to client categories. Turns raw signal into client-ready insight documents.

Coverage Tracking

Media monitoring exports converted to structured tracker format with automatic link resolution. What took hours of manual formatting now runs automatically.

Resource Planning Dashboard

Visual resource allocation across three offices. See who is available, who is overloaded, and where the gaps are before they become problems.

The Result

24-36 Hours freed per week
8 Intelligence signal types
6am Daily automated alerts

The time savings were immediate and measurable. 24 to 36 hours freed per week across the team, the equivalent of a full-time hire. But the real value was in the intelligence layer: the team started spotting opportunities they would have missed entirely, and responding to market signals before competitors even noticed them.
