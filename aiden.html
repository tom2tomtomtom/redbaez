The Phantom System

AIDEN cultivates hundreds of phantom memories from an agency's own culture, documents, team interviews, and ways of working. Each time you talk to it, dozens of phantoms fire from over 400 distinct perspectives the system believes it has experienced in its long "advertising career".

The interesting work comes from the friction, not the variety.

When two phantoms collide on the same brief, the system uses the tension instead of resolving it. Perspectives that stop being useful fade. New ones emerge from breakthroughs. A guardrail stops it ever becoming a yes-machine.
